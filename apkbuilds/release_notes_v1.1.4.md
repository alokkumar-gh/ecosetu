### EcoSetu v1.1.4 Release Notes

#### Highlights:
- **Direct Citizen Offer Review & Counter-Offers**: Full UI workflow for citizens to review all incoming collector offers, place counter-offers, accept winning bids, and auto-dismiss competing offers.
- **Collector Streamlined Make Offer & Doorstep Inspection**: Streamlined instant offer placement for collectors with relaxed photo requirements (doorstep weighing and inspection enabled).
- **Pickup Detail Synchronization & State Normalization**: Robust state derivation between `Pickup` and `CollectionRequest` models across collector and citizen apps with instant start/complete pickup transition workflows.
- **Dynamic Acquisition & Gross Margin Valuation**: Accurate display of negotiated acquisition prices and lot profit margins in collector inventory and receipts.
- **Eco-Saathi AI & Groq Intelligence**: High-speed conversational AI with natural language parsing across en, hi, mr, and or dialects.
