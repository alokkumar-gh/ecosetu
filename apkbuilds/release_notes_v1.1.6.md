### EcoSetu v1.1.6 Release Notes

#### Highlights:
- **Recycler & Collector Enterprise Capabilities**:
  - Direct daily buying rates management and dynamic rate discovery for collectors.
  - Sourcing request broadcast and interactive quote exchange between collectors and recyclers.
  - Recycler authorization certificate / compliance paper verification and lot traceability tracking.
- **Collector Doorstep & Offline Collection**:
  - Support for offline data collection queue and automatic sync when network is restored.
  - Door-to-door collection workflow with real e-waste categorization and multi-angle photo uploads.
  - Voice address / note recording integration and dynamic doorstep price offer counter-negotiation.
- **Admin Verification & System Health**:
  - Live admin verification queue with document preview, approval/rejection actions, and role upgrade pipelines.
  - Dynamic system health analytics, database telemetry, and FCM client synchronization.
- **EcoSaathi AI & Proactive Notifications**:
  - Proactive Hinglish & English status updates for bids, pickups, and green certificates.
  - Real-time FCM push notification client setup and instant broadcast alerts.
